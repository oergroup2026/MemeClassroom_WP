## Section 3: Why Memes in the Classroom? (The Pedagogical Core)

* **Tag**: CORE DIMENSIONS
* **Heading**: Why Memes Belong in the Classroom
* **Subheading**: Memes are not random jokes — they are multimodal cultural languages with immense pedagogical and critical value.

### Pillar 1: Memes as Teaching Tools
* **Icon / Title**: 🎓 Memes as Teaching Tools
* **Description**:
  Memes are multimodal constructions combining image, text, and cultural context. Being familiar and relatable, they lower cognitive load, spark instant engagement, and make abstract concepts concrete.
* **Bullet Points**:
  - Connect academic concepts to familiar cultural language
  - Reinforce recall and assess through student meme creation
  - Lower cognitive barriers for reluctant learners
* **Link**: Explore classroom use cases →

### Pillar 2: Memes as Critical Objects
* **Icon / Title**: 🔍 Memes as Critical Objects
* **Description**:
  Memes are powerful cultural artifacts that shape social narratives and spread rapidly. The classroom is the space to examine their subtext, decode framing, and question hidden agendas.
* **Bullet Points**:
  - Identify bias, rhetoric, and intent embedded in meme structures
  - Distinguish satire from genuine misinformation
  - Develop lifelong critical media analysis habits
* **Link**: Read articles on meme literacy →

## Section 4: The Role of MemeClassroom (Our Role & Philosophy)

* **Tag**: OUR ROLE & PHILOSOPHY
* **Heading**: An Enabler to Learn, Integrate & Reflect
* **Description**:
  MemeClassroom is not a one-stop app that limits where you find content. You are free to discover memes and articles anywhere across the internet. This space exists to help you learn with, on, and about memes, bring them into classrooms with pedagogical intent, and openly share experiences and reflections with fellow educators.

### Card 1: Learn With, On & About Memes
* **Title**: Learn With, On & About Memes
* **Description**:
  Understand memes as multimodal cultural artifacts. Learn how they communicate, why they carry cognitive value, and how to deconstruct their rhetorical framing and hidden agendas.

### Card 2: Pedagogical Integration
* **Title**: Pedagogical Integration
* **Description**:
  Ground your meme usage in real classroom pedagogy. Access beginner courses, real-world use cases, activity guides, and research articles that make abstract concepts accessible and engaging.

### Card 3: Share Experiences & Reflect
* **Title**: Share Experiences & Reflect
* **Description**:
  Connect with educators to exchange what worked in class, reflect on student discussions, remix templates in the Lab, and contribute insights back to a collaborative community.
